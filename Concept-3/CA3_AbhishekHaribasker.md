**10**: Immersion is where the player is completely inside the game world in such a way that they forget that they are playing a game. Embodiment is the feeling that the player character is an extension of our own body, making us think that the actions that the player performs in the game are performed by us at that moment. Eg:- In the game Spider-man Remastered, Immersion would be when we are inside the New York of his world inside the game and Embodiment would be when start thinking of ourselves and our body as that of Peter Parker's, believing that his actions are being performed by us.
